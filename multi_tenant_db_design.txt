tenant_fees
-------------
    - packageCode varchar(25)
    - packageName varchar(100)
    - productCountFrom int
    - productCountTo int
    - registrationFee int
    - monthlyFee int
   Note: All fields are required, packageCode is PK, productCountFrom and productCountTo are jointly unique
   and the productCountFrom and productCountTo ranges are not conflicting (1 to 20, 21-30 correct. But 1 20 20 and 20 to 30 nor correct)

tenant_registration_request
---------------------------
      - tenantBusinessName (required)
      - domainPrefix (required and must be unique)(prefix.myonline.com, Ex: lubuta.myonline.com, kidsnmoms.myonline.com)
      - mailingAddress1 (required)
      - mailingAddress2
      - contactPerson (required)
      - contactNumber1 (required)
      - contactNumber2
      - emailAddress (required)
      - maxInventoryItems (required, integer)
      - packageCode
      - registrationFee (required, integer) -> populated from tenant_fees table
      - monthlyPayment (required, integer)  -> populated from tenant_fees table
      - approvalStatus ('P' for Pending, 'A' for Approved, 'R' for Rejected) Initially its P
      - registrationFeePmtChannel
      - registrationFeePmtRef
      - registrationFeePmtReceipt (BLOB)- screenshot
